How it works

Each camera frame is passed to an image classifier trained on two classes:

  • Danger: obstacles such as trees, walls and power lines, plus unpredictable activity like a backflip on a bike. The drone should stop or steer away.
  • Safe: open space and normal activity such as a person walking. The drone can continue.

Limitation: the model classifies the whole frame. It doesn't locate the obstacle or measure its distance, which a real drone would need from object detection and depth sensors.

Built with Teachable Machine and TensorFlow.js. Everything runs in your browser; no images are uploaded anywhere.
